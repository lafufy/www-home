Common questions

Everything you need to know.

What's the difference between IT Manager and ITSM Manager? +
An IT Manager focuses on the strategic and operational leadership of your technology function — budgets, vendors, staff, infrastructure, and security. An ITSM Manager specialises in IT service management frameworks (like ITIL 4), optimising how IT services are designed, delivered, and improved. Many clients benefit from both.
Can I combine IT Manager and ITSM Manager services? +
Absolutely. We offer a combined track for clients who need both strategic IT leadership and formal ITSM process improvement. Contact us for a bundled pricing proposal — combined engagements typically attract a 15% discount.
How quickly can a fractional manager start? +
Most engagements begin within 48–72 hours of contract signing. We match you with the right senior professional from our network and begin the onboarding sprint immediately.
Are contracts flexible or long-term? +
We operate on rolling monthly contracts with a 30-day notice period. We also offer 6-month and 12-month plans at discounted rates for clients who want guaranteed continuity. There are no penalties for upgrading, downgrading, or adding on services.
What industries do you specialise in? +
Our fractional managers have worked across professional services, FinTech, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, and SaaS. We match you with someone who has relevant domain experience for your industry's specific IT and compliance requirements.
Do you work alongside our existing IT team? +
Yes — fractional managers are designed to lead, coach, and augment your existing team, not replace them. They slot in as senior leadership, filling the strategic gap above day-to-day support staff or junior IT roles.
